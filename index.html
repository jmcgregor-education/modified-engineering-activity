circuit — a loop electricity travels around.
series circuit — one path.
parallel circuit — many paths.
resistance — how much a bulb slows electricity down.

Zap! Electricity comes out of the battery. It goes through the wire. It lights up the bulb. Then it goes back to the battery. It goes around and around.

The loop must be complete. A break stops the electricity. The light goes out.
